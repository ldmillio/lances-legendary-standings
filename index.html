Calculation Explanation

All-play – Each week a team's score is compared with the other 9 teams. A higher score is a win and a lower score is a loss. The record in the table adds those up.

Power ranking – The PR score column is this number, and a higher score is better. It is 50% all-play win rate, 23% lineup edge, and 27% rest-of-season points. Seven of the 15 regular-season weeks are complete, so lineup edge gets 7/15 of the other half and rest-of-season points get 8/15. Those points are Sleeper half-PPR projections for weeks 8–15, before the playoffs, for the best lineup from starters and bench. Bye weeks in that span count as zero.

Start/sit – For each week, the best lineup is the highest-scoring group that roster could have started, using the points each player scored. The column is points scored over weeks 1–7 divided by the points from those best lineups.

Luckiest team – Expected wins are all-play wins divided by 9. Luck is actual wins minus expected wins. The luckiest team is the most wins above that number.

Unluckiest team – Same calculation. The unluckiest team is the most wins below that number.

Wall of Shame – Started a player who was labeled out prior to the game start, or whose team was on a bye.
