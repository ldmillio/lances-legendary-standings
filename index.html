Calculation Explanation

All-play – Each week a team's score is compared with the other 9 teams. A higher score is a win and a lower score is a loss. The record in the table adds those up.

Power ranking – The PR score column is this number, and a higher score is better. It is 50% all-play win rate, 33% lineup edge, and 17% rest-of-season points. Ten of the 15 regular-season weeks are complete, so lineup edge gets 10/15 of the other half and rest-of-season points get 5/15. Those points are Sleeper half-PPR projections for weeks 11–15, before the playoffs, for the best lineup from starters and bench. Bye weeks in that span count as zero.

Start/sit – For each week, the best lineup is the highest-scoring group that roster could have started, using the points each player scored. The column is points scored over weeks 1–10 divided by the points from those best lineups.

Playoff % – The share of 10,000 simulated seasons in which the team finishes in the top 4. The record and points through week 10 stay as they are. For each week from 11 through 15, a team is centered on its best projected lineup from starters and bench, and the opponent is centered on theirs. When the best rostered player for a slot projects zero, that slot is filled from the waiver wire. The fill is the average of the best available players who can start there, up to four. A player who projects less than half of the one just above is left out. The simulated score is drawn around that center using the team's scoring spread from weeks 1–10. Wins come first, then points for. These centers are Sleeper half-PPR projections.

Luckiest team – Expected wins are all-play wins divided by 9. Luck is actual wins minus expected wins. The luckiest team is the most wins above that number.

Unluckiest team – Same calculation. The unluckiest team is the most wins below that number.

Wall of Shame – Started a player who was labeled out prior to the game start, or whose team was on a bye.
